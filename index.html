What we do for you

AI Act · ISO 42001

AI Inventory & Risk Classification

Most companies don't have a clear picture of every AI system they've built, bought, or quietly embedded through a vendor. We help you build and maintain a living inventory of these systems, so you always know what you're running, who's responsible for it, and what regulatory role and risk level it carries — the essential first step before anything else can be assessed.

What we help you with
Inventory of your AI systems — built, bought, or embedded
Role determination and risk classification
Conformity documentation and Declaration of Conformity support
AI Act

Governing Agentic Workflows

Agentic systems make decisions and take actions with less human oversight at every step — which means the compliance questions change too. We help you govern agentic workflows before they become a liability: who's accountable when an agent acts, what guardrails are in place, and how you document it.

What we help you with
Human oversight design and accountability mapping
Guardrails and escalation logic for autonomous decisions
Documentation for agentic system governance
GDPR

Data Governance and Privacy

Every AI system runs on data, and every dataset carries GDPR obligations that don't disappear just because a model is involved. We help you map your data flows, fix the gaps in your legal basis, and put privacy controls in place that hold up under scrutiny — from regulators and from your own customers.

What we help you with
Data mapping and legal basis review
DPIAs and privacy-by-design implementation
Vendor and data processing agreement review
DORA

Operational Resilience

Financial entities and their critical ICT providers now have to prove they can withstand and recover from disruption, not just describe their security posture on paper. We help you build the ICT risk management framework, incident reporting processes and testing program DORA actually requires.

What we help you with
ICT risk management framework design
Incident classification and reporting readiness
Resilience testing and third-party risk oversight
NIS2 · ISO 27001

Security Governance

NIS2 raises the bar on cybersecurity governance — and puts personal liability on management if it's not met. We help you build the security controls, incident response processes and governance structure to meet NIS2 obligations, and get ISO 27001-certified if you need it.

What we help you with
NIS2 gap assessment and governance structure
ISO 27001 implementation and certification support
Incident response planning and testing

About SenecAI6 questions
What is SenecAI?

SenecAI is a compliance partner for startups and SMEs. We bring legal, technical and cybersecurity expertise together in one team, so you can understand which rules apply to your business, close the gaps, and stay compliant as you grow.

Who do you work with?

Mostly startups and SMEs building or using AI — from early-stage teams preparing for investor due diligence to scale-ups closing their first enterprise clients. If digital regulation affects how you build, sell or operate, we can help.

What makes SenecAI different from a law firm or a traditional consultancy?

Most firms give you either a legal opinion or a technical implementation, and leave you to connect the two. Our lawyers, engineers and security specialists work as one team, so the legal analysis turns directly into documentation, architecture and controls you can actually run. We write for founders, not for other lawyers.

Who is on the team?

SenecAI is led by Matei Ștefan (Founder & Legal Consultant), together with Vlad Tudor (Technical Lead) and Marius Petcu (Cybersecurity Lead), supported by a network of partners across AI, cybersecurity and applied research.

Do you only work with companies based in Romania or the EU?

No. We work with companies wherever they are headquartered. What matters is whether you offer products or services in the EU market, not where your office is.

What languages do you work in?

We work in English and Romanian.

Working with us10 questions
How does an engagement work?

Every engagement follows the same four steps:

  1. Discover — we map your regulatory exposure.
  2. Assess — we run a gap analysis against the frameworks that apply to you.
  3. Implement — we do the work: documentation, compliant architecture, security controls.
  4. Monitor — we keep your compliance current as your business and the law evolve.
What ways can we work together?

Three models, matched to what you need:

  • Check & Strategy — hourly consulting for scoping or a second opinion.
  • Project-Based — a fixed scope delivered to a fixed timeline, such as a gap assessment or a certification push.
  • Retainer — ongoing legal, technical and security coverage, effectively a fractional CAIO or CISO without a full-time hire.
How much does it cost?

It depends on your scope and the engagement model. Check & Strategy is billed only for the hours you use; projects are quoted as a fixed price before any work starts; retainers are a predictable monthly fee. After a free intro call, we send you a clear proposal — no surprises.

How long does it take?

A focused question can be answered in days. A gap assessment or a full compliance project typically takes a few weeks to a few months, depending on scope and how quickly we can access the information we need. If you have a hard deadline — a funding round, an enterprise deal, an audit — tell us early and we'll plan milestones around it.

How much of our team's time will this take?

As little as possible. We do the heavy lifting. We'll need access to the right people and documents, mostly during Discover and Assess; after that, most of the work sits with us, with regular check-ins and reviews.

We have a deadline — a funding round or an enterprise deal. Can you help quickly?

Yes. This is one of the most common reasons founders come to us. We can prioritise what investors or procurement teams will actually ask for — due diligence answers, security questionnaires, policies and evidence — and then handle the longer-term work at a sensible pace.

Can you work alongside our existing legal, engineering or security team?

Absolutely. We can lead end to end, or plug in to support your in-house team, external counsel or DPO where they need extra expertise.

Do we keep the documents and policies you produce?

Yes. The policies, documentation, inventories and assessments we produce for you are yours to use in your business.

Is our information kept confidential?

Yes. Everything you share with us is treated as confidential, and we're happy to sign an NDA before any detailed discussion.

Do you offer a guarantee or certification?

No one can honestly guarantee a regulator's or auditor's decision. What we guarantee is rigorous work, a clear view of your residual risks, and documentation that holds up to scrutiny. Where a formal certification is needed (e.g. ISO 27001), we prepare you for the audit, which is carried out by an accredited certification body.
