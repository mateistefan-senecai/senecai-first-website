// The problem, and our promise

Move beyond the AI fluff.

AI is moving fast. New tools, new possibilities, new regulations. And sometimes it feels like everyone else is already one step ahead.

Whether you're just getting started with AI, looking to make better use of it at work, or trying to understand the regulatory landscape, we help you cut through the noise.

From basic AI literacy and practical AI workshops to advanced AI governance, EU AI Act and GDPR training, our programmes are designed around what you actually need to know. Our corporate programmes also come with structured documentation that supports your organisation's obligation under Article 4 of the EU AI Act (as amended by the Digital Omnibus) to take measures for AI literacy.

// The three pillars

Three objectives. One clear path.

Whether you are an individual building future-proof skills or an organisation preparing your teams for an AI-driven environment, our programmes are built around three objectives.

Understand AI

AI literacy & fundamentals
What it is

Practical, jargon-free foundational training that demystifies how AI works, where it fails, and how to use it safely in daily operations. Every corporate programme includes attendance logs, role-relevant curriculum documentation and completion records.

Why it matters

Under Article 4 of the EU AI Act (as amended by the Digital Omnibus), organisations must take measures to support the AI literacy of their staff and anyone operating AI systems on their behalf. What counts is concrete, proportionate action you can document, not a pass mark. Our corporate programmes give you that documentation, while reducing data leaks and “shadow AI” risks.

Who it's for

All employees, non-technical teams, HR and L&D leaders, managers, and individuals looking for a clear, practical foundation in AI.

Govern AI

AI regulation & governance
What it is

In-depth legal, regulatory and operational training on the EU AI Act, how it meets the GDPR, sector-specific rules, and internal AI governance frameworks.

Why it matters

Regulatory missteps carry real financial and reputational costs, while a lack of internal rules stalls innovation. We help you move from abstract legal text to classifying AI systems, managing high-risk obligations and building governance you can defend.

Who it's for

Legal teams, DPOs, compliance and risk officers, executives, product managers, and founders in regulated industries.
