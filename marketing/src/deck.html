Regulations covered

Every regulation, explained.

Reg. (EU) 2024/1689

EU AI Act

Risk-based law for AI systems and GPAI models. Most high-risk duties move to Dec 2027 and Aug 2028 under the 2026 AI Omnibus deal.

Reg. (EU) 2016/679

GDPR

Any processing of personal data, including AI training, inference and log data.

Dir. (EU) 2022/2555

NIS2

Cybersecurity for essential and important entities across 18 sectors. Management can be held personally liable.

Reg. (EU) 2022/2554

DORA

The ICT risk framework for finance, and the ICT providers serving it. In force since 17 January 2025.

Reg. (EU) 2024/2847

Cyber Resilience Act

Security requirements for products with digital elements. Reporting from 11 Sept 2026; most rules from 11 Dec 2027.

ISO/IEC 27001:2022

ISO 27001

The most widely recognised proof of security maturity, and a strong fit for NIS2 and DORA.

ISO/IEC 42001:2023

ISO 42001

The first certifiable AI Management System standard. It complements the AI Act.
