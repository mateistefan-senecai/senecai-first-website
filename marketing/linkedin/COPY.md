Slide copy (already set in the PDF):

1. **Which EU digital rules apply to you?** / The 2026 rulebook for startups & SMEs, in 8 slides. / Swipe →
2. **EU AI Act**: risk-based rules for AI systems and GPAI models. Applies to providers, deployers, importers and distributors, including non-EU companies whose AI is used in the EU. Up to €35M or 7% of turnover.
3. **GDPR**: any processing of personal data of people in the EU, including AI training, inference and log data. Up to €20M or 4% of turnover.
4. **NIS2**: cybersecurity for medium and large "essential" and "important" entities in 18 sectors. Incident early warning within 24h. Management can be held personally liable.
5. **DORA**: ICT risk for banks, insurers, investment firms, payment, e-money and crypto firms, and their ICT providers. Applies since 17 January 2025.
6. **Cyber Resilience Act**: security-by-design for hardware and software with digital elements. Reporting from 11 September 2026; most requirements from 11 December 2027.
7. **ISO 27001 & ISO 42001**: voluntary and certifiable proof that you manage security and AI responsibly. Increasingly requested by enterprise buyers and investors.
8. **One programme, every framework.** Controls built once, mapped to every regulation that applies. / Try the free checker → senecai.eu/resources

> "We're an AI company, GDPR is a separate thing."
>
> It isn't. Training data, inference logs and any personal data your system touches are all

> DPIA or a clearer legal basis for training data.
>
> We map your data flows, fix the gaps in your legal basis and put privacy controls in place
> that hold up under scrutiny, from regulators and from your own customers.
>
> #GDPR #DataProtection #PrivacyByDesign
